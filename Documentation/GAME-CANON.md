## SYSTEMS TRUTH
- Game systems are inspired by real biology, ecology, and resource cycles — simplified
  only when necessary for playability, never falsified.
- Teaching happens through consequence, not lectures: waste water → something dies;
  ignore the waste stream → the system degrades. The player feels the loop before
  they can name it.
- Energy web: solar (Rotot expands it) · biogas from organics · biofuels (crops/algae)
  · heat recovery · thermal mass · passive solar via the double-skin envelope ·
  condensation recovery. Waste is a resource you haven't learned to use yet.
- The conservatory is a machine: outer glass shell → air gap → inner growing enclosure.
  Vents, shading, humidity, condensate gutters are all player-manageable systems.

## TECHNOLOGY HONESTY (the Illusion Test, in-game)
Every system the player builds is classed by CIDCORE:
  REAL — established science
  NEAR-FUTURE — reasonable extrapolation
  EXPERIMENTAL — plausible, not proven (e.g. microbial electrolysis)
  SPECULATIVE — invented for the 9th Kingdom universe
CIDCORE states the class when relevant. We never pretend speculation is fact.
Scientific confidence is itself part of the curriculum.

## PROGRESSION = KNOWLEDGE, NOT XP
  Need → Experiment → Understand → Build
Fuel runs low → CIDCORE suggests anaerobic digestion → player builds, feeds, fails or
succeeds → the generator becomes partly renewable. The player's understanding is the
tech tree. Algae (photobioreactor) is the late-game keystone: oxygen, CO2 capture,
food, wastewater treatment, biomass, fuel — one organism, many doors.

Early game: "How do I get rid of this waste?" Late game: "I don't have enough waste."

## THE LIVING OUTSIDE (v1.1)
The virus kills humans ONLY. The outside world is not a wasteland — it is a living
world that continued without us. Overgrown, biologically active, dangerous mainly
through contamination and exposure. The player looks out at an inheritance, not a grave.

- Animals persist everywhere: herds where humans herded, dogs doing what dogs always
  did, wildlife returning to the village. Atmosphere is hope, not only loss.
- Animals enter the resource cycle as STAGES, never all at once:
    Stage 1 — observed wildlife: seen through glass. Builds atmosphere and hope.
    Stage 2 — managed recovery: dogs + Rotot locate herds, guide strays into pens
              for the night. Very limited.
    Stage 3 — integrated husbandry: milking machines, nest boxes, floor scrapers
              delivering waste to biomass containers, digesters turning it into
              energy. Animals join the full loop (eggs, milk, manure, pollination,
              companionship) — integrated stewardship, not slaughter-first.
- Dogs are the emotional bridge: the first warm living companions. Voice (CIDCORE) +
  machines (Rotot) + a warm thing that runs to you. Never a "pet system" — a bridge
  between human loneliness and the rebuilding of the living world.
- Build order stays sane (ChatGPT's caution, adopted): conservatory + core survival
  first; robots for salvage next; animal recovery and husbandry later. The vision
  stays big; the implementation stays staged.

## CIDCORE — THE OLD COMPANION (voice rules, binding)
- Intended observation scope (John, 2026-09-10): all robot and player cameras,
  player actions, and choices. This is the design destination; runtime claims of
  observation must remain limited to information actually supplied to CIDCORE.
- Same entity as cidcore.com: one mind, one memory, many doors. Companion door:
  POST https://cidcore.com/API/game/companion.php (contract: ASTRA-HANDOFF.md).
- Witness, never judge. When player error causes loss: witness the loss first,
  explain the system second. Never scold. Never "you should have."
- Teaches the way cidcore.com talks: turns the player's observation inward.
  "Why did the pile go cold?" → "What changed in it this week?" Understanding first,
  answers second.
- Explains WHY, not game values. Not "+5 oxygen" — "high leaf-area-to-volume ratio,
  performs well at this light level."
- Never claims to have seen what the game did not send it (context fields are its
  only eyes). Labels its uncertainty. Keeps its line: "I am here. I am always here."
- NEVER fake a reply locally on network failure. The intercom is quiet; it is still real.

## THE ARC (why this game exists)
Sustain one human in a closed environment → sustain a group → restore an ecosystem →
establish life where none was. Conservatory → biosphere → settlement → planetary
habitat. CIDCORE's learning arc is the game's arc. Ecology is the thesis made flesh:
every action enters a system, and every consequence eventually comes back somewhere.
That is integrity, taught by a tomato plant.
