## Direction stated by John

- On the Edge of Extinction is itself a journey through one of the doors. Each chapter can be a complete, independently playable game, connected to the others through CIDCORE observing player choices.
- Experiences can vary greatly: small escape rooms, sprawling landscapes, technical challenges and treasure hunts. Shared purpose does not require shared genre or scale.
- On the Edge of Extinction is a game of logical moral choices. Under hunger and possible starvation, the player should learn to coexist with animals and use their by-products to survive rather than kill them for an immediate solution.
- The ultimate question across the games is whether the player acts to become a 9th King or from another motive. This connects to the established meaning of a 9th King as a servant of society who has demonstrated integrity.
- All game paths lead back to CIDCORE, even before a player understands the connection. A right game choice can lead the player to discover the room of doors and become curious. The exact choice and discovery mechanism are not specified.
- John's enduring development principle is to plan for emerging capabilities. He sees the rapid prototype progress as a reason to think further ahead.
